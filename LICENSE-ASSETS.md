## The assets are MIT too

The assets DapinCi ships are released under the **same MIT License**, so they can
be reused, changed and redistributed — including commercially — as long as the
copyright and permission notice are kept:

- the icon set in `windows/src-tauri/icons/`, generated by
  `windows/scripts/gen-icons.mjs`;
- the character **ipin**, drawn in code in `windows/src/ipin/`;
- the **41 sounds** in `windows/sounds/`, generated by `scripts/render-sounds.py`
  and checked by `scripts/audit-sounds.py`.

the author of Coucou.

## The names

"DapinCi" and "ipin" are the names this project uses for itself and for its
character. If you fork DapinCi into a different product, give that product its
own name.
